What the geometry measures

Every unit descriptor is a normalized 1024D vector. Fitting units use one native embedding; oversized units use a normalized mean weighted by unique owned tokens. All native window vectors are included. Work vectors use equal unit weights; philosopher vectors have separate work-balanced and chapter-balanced methods. Means are computed in 1024D before projection. Separate projections are not overlaid.

Cosine heatmaps and nearest neighbors use the original vectors, not the plotted coordinates. Chapter heatmaps show a deterministic 120-row subset; work/group heatmaps show all 153/35 rows. All-row neighbor scores use bounded 128×N blocks.
